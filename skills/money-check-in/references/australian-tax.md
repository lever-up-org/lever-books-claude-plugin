# Australian tax figures and dates

From ATO pages and ATO search extracts, 15 Nov 2026; check ato.gov.au before relying on a line.
**Check every line again each 1 July.** Checked: **Y** an official ATO page states it; **Y\*** an
official page states it but is the only source or flags it as provisional; **N** not confirmed on
an official page, so point the person to ato.gov.au instead of stating it.

If a question needs a figure that isn't here, don't supply one from memory: name the figure and
point to ato.gov.au or the person's accountant.
